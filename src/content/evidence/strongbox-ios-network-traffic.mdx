## Paywall resources

<Note>**Exh. 9** / counts</Note>

On each first launch after installation, the app downloads paywall resources before any paywall is shown: HAR entries to `user-content.superwalleditor.com`, `fonts.gstatic.com`, `fonts.googleapis.com` and `js.stripe.com`, CONNECT included (C8: the first launch only; its two later CONNECT entries to `js.stripe.com` are left out). On later starts the HAR holds 0 or 1 such entries. In the packet captures, one short connection to each resource host appears on each start of T2 (without Proxyman), on the single start of S18 and A0, and only on the first start of S02, S11b and T3 (with Proxyman); in T2 a `user-content` connection received about 5 kB (report, 3.6). In A8 the paywall webview fetches `js.stripe.com/clover/stripe.js` (1.1 MB); in A5 and C8, at the same time, there are connections to `m.stripe.com` and `m.stripe.network`, recorded as CONNECT only, without content, in the authors' HAR files (not in the published ones).

### A.4 Pseudonymisation and time shift

<p>The labels, the address labels, the masked CloudFront point of presence and the time shift are described in the report ([How it was studied](/publications/strongbox-ios-network-traffic/#2-how-it-was-studied)). The 13-digit <code>sw_cache_key</code> parameter in paywall resource addresses (most likely the publication time of the paywall version on the service side, a hypothesis) cannot be shifted with the run, so it is masked wherever it occurs, with the same placeholder <code>{'<<MASKED_NUM>>'}</code> for every value. The content of paywall resources and the entries of other apps were removed. From the packet captures only extracts are published (DNS, SNI, SYN/QUIC, connection statistics), without content. The TLS, DNS and DNS-answer extracts keep only the hosts matching a pattern, the SNI lists (<code>*_sni-new.txt</code>) only the hosts named in the report and <code>proxyman.com</code>; the SYN/QUIC extracts (<code>*_syn-quic.csv</code>) only the connection starts to the hosts named in the report, and the connection statistics (<code>T2_tcp-conversations.txt</code>) only the conversations with the addresses of those hosts and inside the local network; the rows of other apps were removed. The scripts and their change log are among the files on record (<code>tools/</code>).</p>
